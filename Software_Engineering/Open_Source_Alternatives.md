# List of Free Open Source Software 

- **Libre Office**: Alternative for Microsoft Office.

- **AppFlowy**: Note-taking, organization app similar to notion.

- **ERPnext**: Alternative to Salesforce.

- **MatterMost**: Alternative to Teams.

- **Jitsi**: Alternative to Zoom.

- **Plane**: Alternative to Jira.

- **Krita**: Free Adobe Ilustrator.

- **Blender**: Free After Effects.

- **Ollama**: Local LLM.
